Introduction

By youth. For youth.

Vivid Network exists because teenagers have always had something to say — about identity, culture, friendship, pressure, and what it actually feels like to grow up right now. Most media wasn't built to carry that conversation.

Vivid Network is. It's a home for podcasts, articles, interviews, videos, and creative projects made by young people, for young people — with more of the network still to come.
