What It Is

Vivid Network is a youth-led media platform built for teenagers to tell real stories, share perspectives, explore culture, have honest conversations, and create media about what it's actually like to grow up. It's home to Vivid Voices, our flagship podcast, alongside articles, interviews, and creative work — with more of the network still being built.

Why It Exists

Teenagers are talked about constantly — in research, in headlines, in marketing — but rarely handed the mic to speak for themselves. Vivid Network exists to close that gap: a space built by young people, run on their terms, for their perspective.

Who It's For

Vivid Network is for teenagers navigating identity, friendships, culture, school, and everything in between — and for anyone who wants to actually listen to what that experience sounds like, in their own words.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to give young people a real platform, not a token one. Our vision is a growing network of youth-made media — podcasts, articles, videos, and creative projects — built and shaped by the teenagers who make it.

The Founder
Portrait — Imaan Raza

Hi, I'm Imaan.

I'm a teenager, creator, and the person behind Vivid Network.

I started Vivid Network because I kept noticing the same gap — teenagers have so much to say about growing up, and so few places built to actually hold that conversation. Vivid Network started as an idea to change that: a space where young people could speak for themselves, without it being filtered through someone else's lens.

It began with Vivid Voices, a podcast to start honest conversations with other teenagers. From there, the goal has always been bigger than one show — a full network of youth-made media, growing one story, one episode, and one contributor at a time.

Teenagers should get to talk too.
